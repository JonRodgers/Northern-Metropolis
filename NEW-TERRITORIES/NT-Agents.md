# New Territories - 100 Skilled Business Agents

## Overview

This document outlines 100 specialized AI agents designed to optimize operations for New Territories' STEM and AECo (Architecture, Engineering, Construction) training programs. These agents address key business functions including student management, course administration, scheduling, resource allocation, marketing, and operational efficiency.

---
