## 8-Hour Architecture Workshop (Ages 14-16)

### Workshop Title
**"Design Thinking in Architecture: From Concept to Creation"**

### Workshop Overview
An intensive 8-hour workshop inspired by the Smart Geometry Conference methodology, combining design thinking, hands-on making, digital tools, and real-world problem-solving. Students will experience the complete architectural design process from research to prototype.

### Learning Objectives
- Understand architectural design thinking and problem-solving methodologies
- Develop skills in design representation (sketching, 2D/3D modeling)
- Experience the iterative design process
- Create a functional architectural prototype
- Understand sustainability and human-centered design principles

### Workshop Schedule (8 hours)

#### **Session 1: Design Thinking Foundations (9:00 AM - 10:30 AM, 1.5 hours)**

**Theme**: "What is Architecture? Beyond Buildings"

**Content:**
- Introduction to architectural thinking and design methodology
- Case study: How architects solve real-world problems
- Design thinking framework: Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test
- Interactive discussion: What makes a great space?

**Activities:**
- Icebreaker: "Spaces that Matter" - students share meaningful spaces in their lives
- Design thinking exercise: Identify a problem in the school environment
- Brainstorming session: Generate 10+ solutions to the identified problem
- Group discussion: Evaluate ideas using design criteria

**Materials**: Whiteboards, markers, sticky notes, inspiration images

---

#### **Session 2: Design Representation & Sketching (10:45 AM - 12:15 PM, 1.5 hours)**

**Theme**: "How to Draw Your Ideas"

**Content:**
- Sketching techniques: perspective, orthographic projection, quick ideation
- 2D representation methods: plans, sections, elevations
- Digital tools introduction: SketchUp basics for 3D visualization
- Site analysis and contextual research

**Activities:**
- Sketching intensive: Perspective drawing exercises
- Rapid prototyping: Sketch 5 different design solutions (10 minutes each)
- Digital modeling: Introduction to SketchUp - create a simple 3D form
- Peer critique: Present sketches and receive feedback

**Materials**: Sketch paper, pencils, rulers, laptops with SketchUp, projector

---

#### **Lunch Break (12:15 PM - 1:00 PM, 45 minutes)**

---

#### **Session 3: Materials & Making (1:00 PM - 2:45 PM, 1.75 hours)**

**Theme**: "From Concept to Physical Prototype"

**Content:**
- Material properties and selection for architectural design
- Sustainable materials and environmental responsibility
- Model-building techniques and craftsmanship
- Structural principles and load-bearing systems
- Recycled and organic materials in contemporary architecture

**Activities:**
- Material exploration: Hands-on testing of different materials
- Prototype building: Create a 1:50 scale model of their design solution
- Structural testing: Test models for stability and functionality
- Documentation: Photograph and annotate the design process

**Materials:**
- Cardboard, foam board, wood strips, wire, string
- Recycled materials: plastic bottles, paper, fabric scraps
- Tools: craft knives, cutting mats, glue guns, sandpaper
- Measuring tools: rulers, calipers, levels

---

#### **Session 4: Presentation & Reflection (2:45 PM - 5:00 PM, 2.25 hours)**

**Theme**: "Communicating Your Design"

**Content:**
- Architectural presentation techniques
- Storytelling in design: How to explain your design decisions
- Feedback and iteration in the design process
- Career pathways in architecture and design
- Sustainability and social responsibility in architecture

**Activities:**
- Design presentations: Each student/group presents their solution (5 minutes)
- Peer feedback: Constructive critique using design criteria
- Reflection exercise: What did you learn? What would you change?
- Guest speaker: Practicing architect from atmospheres discussing real-world projects
- Q&A session: Career pathways and design industry insights
- Exhibition setup: Display all models and sketches for school community

**Materials**: Presentation boards, markers, laptops, projector, display stands

---

### Workshop Outcomes
- **Tangible Deliverables**: Physical prototype model, design sketches, digital 3D model
- **Portfolio Pieces**: Photographs and documentation of design process
- **Skills Developed**: Design thinking, sketching, 3D modeling, presentation, teamwork
- **Inspiration**: Exposure to architecture as a career and creative discipline

### Workshop Facilitation
- **Lead Facilitator**: Jonathan Rodgers, CEO/Founding Director, atmospheres
- **Assistant Facilitators**: 2-3 architecture professionals from atmospheres
- **Guest Speaker**: Practicing architect discussing real-world projects
- **Maximum Participants**: 24 students (3 groups of 8)

### Materials & Budget
- **Estimated Cost**: HK$3,500-4,500 (materials, facilitator fees, guest speaker)
- **Setup Requirements**: Large studio space, tables, chairs, power outlets, WiFi
- **Equipment Needed**: Laptops (12-15), projector, screen, cutting mats, tools

## Summer Architecture Course (4 Weeks)

### Course Title
**"Architecture & Sustainability: Designing for Hong Kong's Future"**

### Course Overview
A comprehensive 4-week summer course for ages 14-16, exploring architectural design, sustainability, and urban planning through project-based learning. Students will develop a complete design project from research to presentation, with field visits to iconic Hong Kong buildings and mentorship from practicing architects.

### ESF School Calendar - Summer Holidays
- **Summer Holiday Period**: Mid-June to Late August (approximately 10 weeks)
- **Proposed Course Dates**: July 1-26, 2026 (4 weeks)
- **Schedule**: Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM - 12:30 PM (3.5 hours/day)
- **Total Hours**: 70 hours of instruction

### Course Structure

#### **Week 1: Foundations & Site Analysis**

**Theme**: "Understanding Hong Kong's Built Environment"

**Monday: Course Introduction & Design Thinking**
- Welcome and course overview
- Design thinking methodology
- Introduction to architectural practice
- Activity: "Spaces that Matter" - personal reflection on meaningful spaces
- Assignment: Identify a site in Hong Kong for design project

**Tuesday: Architectural History & Context**
- Hong Kong's architectural evolution
- Colonial, modernist, and contemporary architecture
- Case studies: Iconic Hong Kong buildings (Bank of China Tower, Star Ferry Terminal, etc.)
- Activity: Analyze architectural styles and design principles
- Field visit: Walking tour of Central District

**Wednesday: Site Analysis & Research Methods**
- How architects research and analyze sites
- Urban context, climate, culture, and community
- Mapping and documentation techniques
- Activity: Conduct site analysis of chosen location
- Tools: Photography, sketching, interviews, observation

**Thursday: Sustainability in Architecture**
- Bioclimatic design and environmental responsibility
- Green building principles and certifications
- Case studies: Sustainable architecture in Asia
- Activity: Analyze environmental factors affecting design
- Discussion: Climate change and architectural responsibility

**Friday: Week 1 Review & Project Kickoff**
- Present site analysis findings
- Peer feedback and discussion

- Assignment: Develop design concept sketches
