# ESF Sha Tin College: Introduction to Architecture
## Meeting Agenda – One-Page Brief

---

## What We're Proposing

We want to launch a 12-week architecture course for your 14-16 year-old students. This isn't vocational training—it's about teaching them to think like architects: how to observe the world, solve problems creatively, and understand how design shapes the way we live. Your Design Day showed us the appetite is there. We think we can build something genuinely pioneering.

---

## Why Now? Why This?

The students who came to Design Day were hungry for it. They want to understand how buildings work, why cities are designed the way they are, and what it means to create spaces that matter. Right now, there's no accredited secondary school architecture course in Hong Kong—potentially nowhere in the world outside university summer programs. We have a chance to be first.

---

## What the Course Actually Does

**Weeks 1-2:** We start with design thinking. How do architects approach problems? What makes a space feel right? We'll walk through Sha Tin, analyze buildings, and get students sketching.

**Weeks 3-4:** They learn to draw and model. Sketches, plans, sections, 3D models—both by hand and on screen. We'll use SketchUp and introduce them to how architects actually communicate ideas.

**Weeks 5-6:** Bioclimatic design and sustainability. Hong Kong's climate is brutal—heat, humidity, typhoons. We'll visit the HSBC building (Foster + Partners) to see how real architects solve these problems. Students design a climate-responsive pavilion for your campus.

**Weeks 7-8:** How buildings actually stand up. Materials, structures, loads. They'll build models, test them, understand why concrete behaves differently from steel. We'll bring in a structural engineer to show them real projects.

**Weeks 9-10:** Urban design and community. We analyze Sha Tin's streets, public spaces, heritage sites. Students identify a real community need and design a response—a plaza, a community center, something that matters.

**Weeks 11-12:** Capstone projects. Each student presents a comprehensive design to practicing architects. We invite professionals in to mentor them, critique their work, and talk about what architecture actually is as a career.

---

## The STEAM Connection

This isn't just art class. We're weaving in:
- **Physics & Engineering:** How structures work, load paths, material properties
- **Chemistry:** Concrete, sustainable materials, environmental science
- **Mathematics:** Geometry, proportions, calculations, data analysis
- **Technology:** Digital design tools, 3D printing, building systems
- **Art & Design:** Composition, spatial thinking, aesthetics, craft

Every project connects the dots between these disciplines through real design challenges.

---

## Who Teaches It?

We bring in practicing architects as mentors and guest speakers. Students get one-on-one guidance from professionals working on real projects. We're talking to firms like Foster + Partners, local practices, structural engineers, sustainability consultants. The course becomes a bridge between your school and the design community.

---

## Field Trips & Real Places

We take students to:
- **HSBC Headquarters** – See climate-responsive design in action
- **Sustainable buildings in Hong Kong** – Green building standards, renewable energy
- **Sha Tin's heritage sites** – Tian Hou Temple, understanding cultural architecture
- **Architectural practices** – See how architects actually work
- **Urban spaces** – Analyze what makes public spaces work or fail

---

## What Students Walk Away With

By the end, they'll have:
- A portfolio of design work showing their thinking process
- Real skills in sketching, modeling, and digital design
- Deep understanding of how buildings respond to climate and community
- Connections with practicing architects and mentors
- Clarity about whether architecture is for them—or what related fields might be
- Most importantly: a way of thinking about the world that's useful whether they become architects or not

---

## Practical Details

**Duration:** 12 weeks (one semester) or extended to full year  
**Class size:** 15-20 students (hands-on work needs smaller groups)  
**Time commitment:** 3-4 hours per week  
**Space needed:** Studio space for models, digital access, field trip logistics  
**Materials:** Sketching supplies, model-making materials, software (mostly free)  
**Cost:** Modest—mainly materials and guest speaker honorariums

## Why This Matters

Architecture teaches you to observe carefully, think systematically, and create solutions that serve real people. Those skills matter whether students become architects, engineers, urban planners, product designers, or anything else. We're not just teaching them about buildings—we're teaching them a way of thinking.

And honestly, we think it could set a precedent. If this works at Sha Tin, other schools will want to do it. You'd be pioneering something genuinely new in Hong Kong education.
