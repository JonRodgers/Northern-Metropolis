## Conflict of Interest Policy

### Disclosure Requirements
- All co-founders must disclose any external business interests
- Annual conflict of interest declarations required
- Board approval required for any external business activities

### Restrictions
- No competing tequila businesses without board approval
- No direct sales to competitors
- No solicitation of company employees or partners
- Non-compete clause: 2 years post-departure

---

## Voting Rights & Governance

### Board Voting
- Each director has one vote
- Majority vote required for board decisions
- Tie-breaking vote: Managing Director (Jonathan Rodgers)
- Quorum: 3 of 4 directors required

### Shareholder Voting
- Voting rights proportional to equity stake
- Major decisions require 75% shareholder approval:
  - Equity issuance or dilution
  - Sale or merger of company
  - Liquidation or dissolution
  - Amendment to articles of association

### Dispute Resolution
- Internal mediation (30 days)
- Arbitration (if mediation fails)
- Jurisdiction: Hong Kong

---

## Exit & Buyout Provisions

### Voluntary Exit
- 6-month notice required
- Equity subject to vesting schedule
- Unvested equity forfeited
- Non-compete clause applies (2 years)

### Involuntary Termination
- Severance: 3 months salary
- Equity subject to vesting schedule
- Unvested equity forfeited
- Non-compete clause applies (2 years)

### Buyout Valuation
- Based on annual financial performance
- Valuation formula: 3x EBITDA (or agreed multiple)
- Payment terms: 12-24 months
- Right of first refusal for remaining shareholders

### Death or Disability
- Equity passes to estate/beneficiaries
- Company has option to repurchase at fair market value
- 12-month payment terms
