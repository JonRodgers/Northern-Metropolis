# ISO 19650

## Overview

ISO 19650 is an international standard for managing information over the whole life cycle of built assets using Building Information Modelling (BIM).

## Key Components

### Part 1: Concepts and Principles
- Establishes the foundational concepts and principles for information management in BIM
- Defines roles, responsibilities, and competencies
- Sets out the framework for information requirements and delivery

### Part 2: Information Delivery
- Specifies the processes and procedures for information delivery
- Covers the appointment of team members and information management roles
- Details the information requirements and information delivery specifications

### Part 3: Information Delivery Specification
- Provides guidance on developing information delivery specifications
- Addresses the technical aspects of information exchange
- Covers data formats and standards

## Core Principles

- **Information Management**: Structured approach to managing information throughout the asset lifecycle
- **Collaboration**: Emphasis on collaborative working between all parties
- **Standardization**: Use of standardized processes and formats
- **Accountability**: Clear definition of roles and responsibilities

## Applications in AEC

- Project planning and delivery
- Asset management
- Facility operations
- Maintenance and refurbishment

## Related Standards

- ISO 19650-1: Concepts and principles
- ISO 19650-2: Information delivery
- ISO 19650-3: Information delivery specification
- ISO 19650-5: Security-minded approach to information management
