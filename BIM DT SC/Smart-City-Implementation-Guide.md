### Incident Management

**Incident Response Procedure**
1. [ ] Detect incident (automated alert or manual report)
2. [ ] Assess severity (critical, high, medium, low)
3. [ ] Notify incident commander
4. [ ] Assemble response team
5. [ ] Investigate root cause
6. [ ] Implement temporary fix
7. [ ] Implement permanent fix
8. [ ] Verify resolution
9. [ ] Document incident
10. [ ] Conduct post-mortem
11. [ ] Implement preventive measures
12. [ ] Close incident

**Incident Severity Levels**
- [ ] Critical: System down, safety risk, major impact
- [ ] High: Significant degradation, important service affected
- [ ] Medium: Minor degradation, workaround available
- [ ] Low: Cosmetic issue, no impact on service

**Response Time SLAs**
- [ ] Critical: 15 minutes
- [ ] High: 1 hour
- [ ] Medium: 4 hours
- [ ] Low: 24 hours
