# REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)
## Lane Crawford New Food Concept - Design & Implementation

**Date:** February 5, 2026  
**RFP Reference:** LC-FC-2026-001  
**Submission Deadline:** February 19, 2026, 5:00 PM HKT

---

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Lane Crawford is seeking proposals from qualified architectural and design firms for the development and implementation of a new food retail and F&B concept across four of our Hong Kong stores. This innovative concept will blend fashion, culture, and flavors to create dynamic, trend-driven spaces that appeal to a new generation of customers while maintaining our luxury positioning.

### Project Scope
- Development of a unified design concept adaptable to four distinct locations
- Creation of integrated food retail and light F&B spaces
- Implementation of highly modular fixtures and display systems
- Coordination of all statutory submissions and licensing requirements
- Management of the design and construction process from concept to completion

### Project Locations
1. **Lane Crawford IFC** - 4,204 sq ft (Retail: 3,004 sq ft / F&B: 1,200 sq ft)
2. **Lane Crawford Pacific Place** - 1,363 sq ft (Retail: 863 sq ft / F&B: 500 sq ft)
3. **Lane Crawford Times Square** - 2,741 sq ft (Retail: 1,741 sq ft / F&B: 1,000 sq ft)
4. **Lane Crawford Canton Road** - 2,191 sq ft (Retail: 1,228 sq ft / F&B: 963 sq ft)

### Project Timeline
The project must be completed according to the following schedule:
- Concept Design: February - May 2026
- Technical Development: March - July 2026
- Implementation: May - September 2026
- Final Completion: September 30, 2026

## 2. SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Interested firms must submit a comprehensive proposal addressing the following:

### 2.1 Firm Experience & Qualifications
- Company profile and history
- Previous relevant projects in luxury retail and F&B environments
- Specific experience with projects in IFC, Pacific Place, Times Square, and Harbour City
- Experience with fast-track projects of similar complexity
- Professional registrations and certifications (including AP status)

### 2.2 Project Team
- Organizational chart showing key team members and reporting structure
- Resumes of key personnel, including design lead, project director, and technical director
- Identification of Authorized Person (AP) and other statutory consultants
- Proposed sub-consultants (MEP, kitchen, fire safety, etc.)
- Staffing plan showing resource allocation across all four sites

### 2.3 Design Approach
- Initial design concept and vision for the project
- Approach to creating a unified design language adaptable to different locations
- Strategy for addressing the unique technical requirements of each mall
- Proposed materials, finishes, and fixture systems
- Approach to lighting, signage, and environmental graphics

### 2.4 Technical Approach
- Methodology for addressing statutory requirements (FEHD, FSD, etc.)
- Strategy for expediting licensing approvals
- Approach to MEP coordination and integration
- Fire safety compliance strategy
- Acoustic and odor control solutions

### 2.5 Project Management
- Detailed project schedule showing all key milestones
- Resource allocation plan across all four sites
- Quality control procedures
- Risk management strategy
- Communication and reporting protocols

### 2.6 Fee Proposal
- Detailed breakdown of professional fees by phase and discipline
- Schedule of reimbursable expenses
- Hourly rates for additional services
- Proposed payment schedule
- List of exclusions or assumptions

## 4. LANDLORD-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

Proposals must demonstrate understanding of and compliance with the specific technical requirements of each mall landlord:

### 4.1 IFC (Managed by International Finance Centre Management Co., Ltd.)
- Compliance with IFC Mall Technical Manual (2025 Edition)
- Strategy for addressing IFC's enhanced smoke extraction requirements
- Approach to MEP coordination with central building systems
- Plan for managing construction logistics within IFC's restricted working hours
- Compliance with IFC's sustainable design guidelines

### 4.2 Pacific Place (Managed by Swire Properties)
- Compliance with Pacific Place Fit-out Guide (2025 Edition)
- Strategy for addressing Pacific Place's strict acoustics requirements
- Approach to odor control and ventilation limitations
- Plan for managing construction access through the mall's designated routes
- Compliance with Swire Properties' Sustainable Development Policy

### 4.3 Times Square (Managed by Times Square Limited)
- Compliance with Times Square Technical Guidelines (2025 Edition)
- Strategy for vertical transportation of construction materials and waste
- Approach to shared grease trap management
- Plan for after-hours construction work
- Compliance with Times Square's energy efficiency requirements

### 4.4 Harbour City (Managed by The Wharf (Holdings) Limited)
- Compliance with Harbour City Tenant Fit-out Manual (2025 Edition)
- Strategy for integration with Harbour City's Fire Service Installation network
- Approach to managing complex logistics for night-work
- Plan for hoarding design and customer communication
- Compliance with Wharf's environmental management system
