## 4. APPROACH & METHODOLOGY

Our approach to delivering Lane Crawford's new food concept is structured around four key phases:

### Phase 1: Concept Development (Weeks 1-6)
- Immersive workshop with Lane Crawford team to deeply understand the brand vision
- Development of unified design language that can be adapted to each location
- Creation of modular fixture and display systems that can be efficiently implemented across all sites
- Presentation of concept design, including 3D visualizations and material palettes

### Phase 2: Technical Development & Submissions (Weeks 7-12)
- Parallel development of detailed design packages for all four locations
- Early engagement with mall landlords to identify and address technical requirements
- Preparation of licensing documentation for FEHD and FSD submissions
- Development of comprehensive FF&E specifications and custom fixture designs

### Phase 3: Tender & Procurement (Weeks 13-16)
- Preparation of tender documentation for contractor selection
- Sourcing of specialty materials and custom fixtures
- Coordination with kitchen equipment suppliers and MEP consultants
- Value engineering to optimize budget without compromising design intent

### Phase 4: Implementation & Handover (Weeks 17-32)
- Phased construction supervision across all four sites
- Regular coordination meetings with contractors and mall management
- Quality control inspections at key milestones
- Commissioning and handover of completed spaces

### Innovative Approaches
- **Digital Twin Technology:** We will create a digital twin of each space to simulate customer flow, test operational efficiency, and visualize design adaptations
- **Modular Design System:** Our approach focuses on creating a kit-of-parts that can be efficiently adapted to each location while maintaining brand consistency
- **Licensing Fast-Track:** Our in-house AP and licensing specialists will utilize the Professional Certification System (PCS) to expedite approvals
