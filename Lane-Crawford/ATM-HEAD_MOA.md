# MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
## Between HEAD Architects and Atmospheres Limited

**Date:** February 5, 2026

---

## 1. PARTIES

This Memorandum of Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**HEAD Architects** ("Client"), an architectural firm with its principal place of business at Unit 1201, Tai Sang Commercial Building, 24-34 Hennessy Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong, represented by its Design Director, Mike Atkin;

and

**Atmospheres Limited** ("Consultant"), a design consultancy with its principal place of business at Unit 1502, The Center, 99 Queen's Road Central, Hong Kong, represented by its Principal, Jonathan Rodgers.

(collectively referred to as the "Parties")

## 2. PURPOSE

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms and conditions under which the Consultant will provide design services to the Client as a sub-contractor for the Lane Crawford New Food Concept project (the "Project") across four Hong Kong locations: IFC, Pacific Place, Times Square, and Canton Road.

## 3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

### 3.1 Project Description
The Project involves the design and implementation of a new food retail and F&B concept for Lane Crawford across four Hong Kong locations, with a total area of approximately 10,499 square feet. The concept will blend fashion, culture, and flavors to create dynamic, trend-driven spaces that appeal to a new generation of customers while maintaining Lane Crawford's luxury positioning.

### 3.2 Consultant's Responsibilities
The Consultant shall provide the following services under the guidance of the Client:

- Develop conceptual design and creative direction
- Create brand identity and visual language for the concept
- Develop customer experience strategy
- Design environmental graphics, signage, and digital touchpoints
- Provide art direction for all visual elements
- Recommend materials and finish selections
- Prepare presentation materials for client meetings
- Attend key client meetings and presentations
- Provide design support during implementation phase
- Collaborate with the Client's technical team

### 3.3 Client's Responsibilities
The Client shall:

- Serve as the primary contract holder with Lane Crawford
- Provide overall project management and coordination
- Provide technical design and implementation oversight
- Manage all statutory submissions and licensing requirements
- Coordinate with mall landlords and technical consultants
- Develop construction documentation
- Oversee contractor selection and construction administration
- Manage project timeline and budget
- Lead client communications and reporting
- Provide the Consultant with necessary project information and documentation

### 3.4 Deliverables
The Consultant shall provide the following deliverables:

- Concept design presentation
- Brand identity guidelines
- Space planning diagrams
- Design development drawings
- Material and finish specifications
- Environmental graphics and signage design
- 3D visualizations and renderings
- Presentation materials for client meetings
- Design intent documentation

## 4. FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENTS

### 4.1 Fee Structure
The total professional fee payable to the Consultant for the services described herein is HK$2,024,312.

### 4.2 Payment Schedule
The Client shall pay the Consultant according to the following schedule:
1. 10% upon signing of this Agreement (HK$202,431)
2. 15% upon completion of concept design (HK$303,647)
3. 25% upon submission of design development package (HK$506,078)
4. 20% upon approval of final design (HK$404,862)
5. 20% upon commencement of construction (HK$404,862)
6. 10% upon project completion (HK$202,431)

### 4.3 Expenses
Reimbursable expenses shall be billed at cost plus 5% administrative fee. All expenses exceeding HK$5,000 require prior written approval from the Client.

### 4.4 Payment Terms
Invoices shall be paid within 30 days of receipt. Late payments shall incur interest at a rate of 1.5% per month on the outstanding amount.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 5.1 Ownership
All intellectual property created by the Consultant specifically for the Project shall be assigned to the Client upon full payment of fees, who will in turn assign it to Lane Crawford as stipulated in the main contract.

### 5.2 Pre-existing Intellectual Property
The Consultant shall retain ownership of any pre-existing intellectual property brought to the Project.

### 5.3 Portfolio Rights
The Consultant shall have the right to include the Project in its portfolio, website, and other promotional materials, with appropriate credit given to the Client and Lane Crawford, subject to Lane Crawford's approval.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 6.1 Term
This Agreement shall commence on the date of signing and shall continue until the completion of the Project, estimated to be September 30, 2026, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

### 6.2 Termination
This Agreement may be terminated:
- By mutual written agreement of the Parties
- By the Client upon 30 days' written notice to the Consultant
- By either Party in the event of a material breach by the other Party, which remains uncured for 14 days after written notice
- Automatically if the main contract between the Client and Lane Crawford is terminated

### 6.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, the Consultant shall:
- Complete all work in progress up to the date of termination
- Deliver all completed and in-progress deliverables to the Client
- Submit a final invoice for services rendered up to the date of termination
- Return all confidential information and materials provided by the Client or Lane Crawford

The Client shall pay the Consultant for all services properly performed up to the date of termination.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

### 7.1 Confidential Information
The Consultant agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all information related to the Project, including but not limited to design concepts, business strategies, and financial information of both the Client and Lane Crawford.

### 7.2 Duration
The confidentiality obligations shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

### 7.3 Non-Disclosure
The Consultant shall not disclose any confidential information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Client, except as required by law or court order.

## 8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 8.1 Amicable Resolution
The Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement through amicable discussions.

### 8.2 Mediation
If the dispute cannot be resolved through discussions within 30 days, the Parties agree to submit the dispute to mediation in Hong Kong in accordance with the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre Mediation Rules.

### 8.3 Arbitration
If mediation fails to resolve the dispute within 60 days of the appointment of the mediator, the dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in Hong Kong in accordance with the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre Administered Arbitration Rules.

## 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 9.1 Relationship of Parties
The Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or partner of the Client. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture between the Parties.

### 9.2 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, negotiations, and discussions.

### 9.3 Amendments
Any amendments to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

### 9.4 Non-Assignment
Neither Party may assign this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other Party.

### 9.5 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

### 9.6 Force Majeure
Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, natural disasters, pandemic, war, terrorism, riots, or government actions.

### 9.7 Notices
All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, registered mail, or email with confirmation of receipt to the addresses specified in this Agreement.

### 9.8 Counterparts
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
