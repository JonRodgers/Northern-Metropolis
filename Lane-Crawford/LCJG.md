
PAO & WOO EMPIRE

As of **February 2026**, the net worth of **Peter Woo**, the patriarch of the Woo family, is estimated at approximately **US$11.7 billion**. Some tracking indices, such as the Bloomberg Billionaires Index, have historically valued the family’s collective wealth higher, reaching up to **US$18 billion**. The family's fortune is primarily managed through the privately held flagship **Wheelock and Company**, which controls a massive real estate and logistics empire in Hong Kong and Mainland China. 

Business Portfolio and Asset Breakdown

The Woo family's wealth is structured through a hierarchy of listed and private entities. Following the privatization of **Wheelock and Company** in **2020**, the family holds direct controlling interests in two major publicly traded subsidiaries. 

1. Real Estate & Investment (Wharf REIC)

The most valuable part of the portfolio is managed by **Wharf Real Estate Investment Company (Wharf REIC)**, which focuses on premium "trophy" assets in Hong Kong. 

- **Harbour City:** A massive mixed-use complex in Tsim Sha Tsui, valued at approximately **HK$149.8 billion** as of late 2025.
- **Times Square:** A major retail and office landmark in Causeway Bay, valued at **HK$42 billion**.
- **Plaza Hollywood:** A regional shopping mall in Diamond Hill.
- **Luxury Residential:** Includes high-profile developments like **Mount Nicholson** on The Peak, known for record-breaking sale prices. 

2. Diversified Operations (The Wharf Holdings)

**The Wharf (Holdings) Ltd.** manages a broader range of development and infrastructure projects. 

- **Hong Kong Development:** Managed by **Wheelock Properties**, with over **1,000 units** slated for launch in **2026**, including projects in Wong Chuk Hang and Sheung Shui.
- **China Investment Properties:** Large-scale "International Finance Square" (IFS) developments in mainland cities like Chengdu, Changsha, and Chongqing.
- **Logistics:** Operates **Modern Terminals**, a major container terminal operator in Hong Kong and the Pearl River Delta.
- **Hotels:** Includes the **Marco Polo Hotels** brand and the luxury **Niccolo Hotels**. 

3. Private Retail and Global Equity

- **Lane Crawford Joyce Group:** A privately held luxury retail empire encompassing **Lane Crawford** department stores and **Joyce** boutiques.
- **Salvatore Ferragamo S.p.A.:** The family maintains a **6% stake** in the Italian luxury shoemaker.
- **Longfor Group:** A **6% stake** in the Beijing-based property developer. 

Individual Asset Estimates

- **Peter Woo:** Directly controls the family’s majority stakes in Wheelock and its subsidiaries. His net worth is frequently cited between **US$11.7 billion** and **US$13 billion** based on market fluctuations of Wharf REIC and Wharf Holdings.
- **Douglas Woo (Son):** Currently serves as the Chairman of Wheelock and Company. While much of his wealth is tied to the family trust, he has made individual luxury acquisitions, such as a **HK$59.8 million** flat in Repulse Bay in **2023**.
- **Jennifer Woo (Daughter):** Leads the **Lane Crawford Joyce Group**, managing the family’s extensive private retail operations


**Wharf Estate** (officially **Wharf Estates Limited**) is the primary property investment arm of **Wharf Real Estate Investment Company Limited** **(Wharf REIC)**. It manages a multi-billion dollar portfolio of "trophy" assets that are among the most valuable in Hong Kong and the region. 

Key Property Portfolio

The estate is defined by six premier "blue-chip" assets in Hong Kong and strategic commercial holdings in Singapore. 

- **Harbour City (Tsim Sha Tsui):** The flagship of the portfolio and Hong Kong's largest retail-office-hotel complex.
    - **Valuation:** **HK$149.8 billion** (as of mid-2025).
    - **Components:** Spans over **8 million square feet**, including the Ocean Terminal, Ocean Centre, and Gateway complexes. It typically contributes about **70%** of the group's total revenue.
- **Times Square (Causeway Bay):** A major landmark integrated with the MTR station, featuring high-end retail and two Grade-A office towers.
    - **Valuation:** **HK$42.0 billion**.
- **Plaza Hollywood (Diamond Hill):** A major shopping mall in Kowloon East catering to the mid-market segment.
- **Central Business District Assets:** Includes **Wheelock House** and **Crawford House**, providing premium office and retail space in Hong Kong's financial heart.
- **The Murray (Central):** A luxury hotel converted from a landmark government building.
- **Singapore Assets:** Managed via **Wharf Estates Singapore**, including **Wheelock Place** and **Scotts Square** on Orchard Road. 

Financial & Strategic Position

- **Total Portfolio Value:** The total investment property (IP) assets of Wharf REIC were valued at approximately **HK$217.2 billion** as of June 30, 2025.
- **Leasehold Advantage:** A unique strength of the portfolio is that many core assets are held under rare **999-year leases**, providing exceptional long-term stability.
- **Revenue Generation:** The estate's primary income is derived from **rental revenue**, which totaled **HK$10.8 billion** for the full year 2024.
- **Market Share:** Together, Harbour City and Times Square historically account for nearly **8% to 10%** of Hong Kong's total retail sales. 

Corporate Structure

Wharf REIC was spun off from **The Wharf (Holdings) Limited** in 2017 to create a focused platform for stable, income-generating investment properties. It is now a subsidiary of the private Wheelock and Company Limited, ultimately controlled by the **Woo family**.
