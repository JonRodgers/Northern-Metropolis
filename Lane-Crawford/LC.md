### Fee Structure Considerations
Fees are driven by the complexity of simultaneous mall submissions and the aggressive 6-8 month timeline.

#### 1. Estimated Design Fee Breakdown
For a 10,500 sq. ft. total scope (approx. 2,625 sq. ft. per site) with a unifying modular design and four distinct submissions:
- **Lump Sum Design Fee:** Reputable firms in Hong Kong typically charge HK$300-HK$800 per sq. ft. for high-end retail/F&B.
- **Base Calculation:** 10,500 sq. ft. × HK$450 (mid-range) = HK$4.7M.
- **Modular Design Discount:** Since there is one unifying design, a "master concept fee" is often charged for the first site, with a 30%-50% discount on the design portion for subsequent "rollout" sites.
- **Final Range:** Expect a total fee between HK$3.5M and HK$6.5M, depending on firm seniority.

#### 2. Premium for Aggressive Timelines
An 8-month timeline from concept to launch is considered "fast-track" in Hong Kong, given the stringent submission requirements for Grade A malls.
- **Rush Premium:** Firms often add 15%-25% to their standard fee to cover overtime for parallel processing of four separate technical submissions (Building Department, Fire Services, and FEHD licensing).
- **Parallel Submission Management:** You are paying for the capacity to handle four different landlord approval processes simultaneously, which requires dedicated teams for each site.

#### 3. Construction & Licensing Context
- **Fit-out Costs:** Luxury F&B in these malls can range from HK$2,500 to HK$4,500 per sq. ft. for construction.
- **Submission Fees:** Professional fees usually include coordination with Registered Structural Engineers (RSE) and Authorized Persons (AP) for statutory submissions, but third-party consultant fees (e.g., MEP, Kitchen Consultants) are typically billed separately or as a 10%-15% management markup on their sub-contracts.

#### 4. Recommended Payment Schedule
Given the aggressive schedule, use a milestone-heavy structure:
1. **Concept Design (Unifying Modular):** 20%
2. **Detailed Design & Mall Submissions (4 sites):** 30%
3. **Tendering & Contractor Award:** 15%
4. **Construction Supervision (Practical Completion):** 25%
5. **Defects Liability / Final Account:** 10%

### Licensing Requirements
Operating F&B stalls across these four premier malls requires navigating a dual-track process: **Government Statutory Compliance** and **Landlord/Mall Specific Requirements**.

#### 1. Core Statutory Licenses (FEHD)
Most "light F&B" stalls in department stores fall under the [**Light Refreshment Restaurant (LRR) License**](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/licensing/Guide_on_Types_of_Licences_Required.html).
- **Application Submission:** Submit three copies of proposed layout plans (scale ≥ 1:100) to the [**Food and Environmental Hygiene Department (FEHD)**](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/licensing/Quick_Reference_Guide.pdf).
- **Provisional License Strategy:** To meet the 6-8 month timeline, aim for a **Provisional License**. This is typically issued within one working day of submitting certificates from an Authorized Person (AP) or Registered Structural Engineer (RSE) confirming all essential health, building, and fire safety requirements are met.
- **Professional Certification System (PCS):** As of March 2023, the [**PCS option**](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/licensing/images/FEHD.pdf) for the full license bypasses the initial verification inspection to speed up the process.
- **Kitchen & Hygiene:** Requirements include a separate ventilating system for kitchens, grease traps, and the nomination of a [**Hygiene Manager/Supervisor**](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/howtoseries/forms/new/FF.pdf).

#### 2. Fire Safety Requirements (FSD)
The [**Fire Services Department (FSD)**](https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/fire_protection/licensing/premise_pe.html) issues a **Fire Services Certificate**, a prerequisite for any FEHD license.
- **Installation Standards:** Stalls must comply with FSD standards for [**portable fire extinguishers**](https://www.buildingmgt.gov.hk/en/Daily_Operation_of_Building_Management/6_7_2.html), manual fire alarms, and emergency lighting.
- **Modular Constraints:** For a modular design across four malls, ensure all combustible materials (partitions, ceilings) meet [**BS 476 Part 7 Class 1 or 2**](https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/source/licensing/PPA_101A_EN_201006.pdf) standards for surface spread of flame.
- **Electronic Submission (e-FSR):** Use the Electronic Fire Safety Requirements system to receive requirements and submit completion reports via email to expedite approvals.

#### 3. Mall-Specific "Hidden" Hurdles
While FEHD/FSD rules are standard, each mall (Wharf, Swire, SHKP) has its own **Technical Fit-out Guide**:

| **Mall (Location)**           | **Key Statutory/Mall Consideration**                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **IFC (Central)**             | Stringent MEP coordination; high-spec smoke extraction requirements often exceed FSD minimums.    |
| **Pacific Place (Admiralty)** | Known for strict acoustics and smell-mitigation (EPD) controls to protect nearby luxury retail.   |
| **Harbour City (TST)**        | Complex logistics for night-work and massive Fire Service Installation (FSI) network integration. |
| **Times Square (CWB)**        | Focus on vertical transportation of waste and strict adherence to shared grease-trap capacities.  |

#### 4. Critical Timeline Milestones
To hit a **6-month launch**:
- **Month 1-2:** Finalize modular design; concurrently submit "Initial Plans" to Mall Management for approval.
- **Month 3:** Formal FEHD/FSD submission. Use an AP/RSE to prepare [**Certificates of Compliance**](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/licensing/guide.html).
- **Month 5:** Obtain **Provisional License** to start soft-launch operations while the "Full License" audit is pending.

### Project Delivery Team Requirements
To deliver four luxury F&B projects in Hong Kong on a 6-8 month fast-track timeline, a single, highly coordinated **Project Delivery Team** is essential.

#### 1. Core Design & Creative Resources
- **Design Lead (Creative Director):** Responsible for the "one unifying modular design" and ensuring brand consistency across all four premium locations.
- **Lighting Consultant:** Crucial for "luxury" positioning. They handle lighting layouts, lux calculations for mall approvals, and mood settings to match high-end mall standards.
- **Senior Interior Designers (x2):** At least two senior designers are needed to handle the volume of four parallel sets of technical drawings and material boards.

#### 2. Statutory & Licensing Resources (Legal Prerequisites)
- **Authorized Person (AP):** Legally required to certify building safety and sign off on [**FEHD/Buildings Department submissions**](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/howtoseries/forms/new/A_Guide_to_Restaurant.PDF).
- **Registered Structural Engineer (RSE):** Required if your "modular design" involves heavy fixtures or structural changes (e.g., new kitchen partitions or ceiling supports).
- **Licensing Consultant:** A specialist to manage the administrative [**FEHD (Food and Environmental Hygiene Department)**](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/licensing/images/FEHD.pdf) application process, ensuring your Provisional License is secured in time for the 6-month launch.

#### 3. Technical & Engineering Resources
- **MEP Consultant (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing):** Essential for F&B to handle grease traps, ventilation (to [**FSD/EPD standards**](https://fdbhk.com/mep-and-licensing-consultancy/)), and fire service integration.
- **Kitchen Consultant:** Specialized in designing efficient F&B workflows, selecting professional-grade equipment, and ensuring the kitchen layout passes FEHD hygiene inspections.
- **Fire Services Consultant/Registered Fire Engineer:** To ensure all four sites meet mall-specific and [**FSD (Fire Services Department)**](https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/fire_protection/licensing/premise_pe.html) fire safety requirements for the Fire Services Certificate.

#### 4. Project Management & Execution
- **Project Manager (PM):** The "central engine" who coordinates with four different mall landlords, manages the aggressive 8-month timeline, and oversees the contractor.
- **Quantity Surveyor (QS):** Manages the budget across four sites, handles tendering for a single contractor to build the modular design, and controls [**fit-out costs**](https://www.hellotoby.com/en/hire/business/restaurant-interior-design-renovation).
