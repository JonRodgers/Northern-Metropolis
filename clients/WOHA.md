# Introductory Letter to WOHA and Wong Mun Summ

## About WOHA

WOHA is a leading architecture firm founded in 1994 by **Mun Summ Wong**, who serves as Founding Director. The practice is recognized for its innovative approach to sustainable and integrated design, with a portfolio that includes iconic projects such as Parkroyal Collection Pickering, Oasia Hotel Downtown, and Kampung Admiralty. Mun Summ Wong holds the position of Professor in Practice at the National University of Singapore's Department of Architecture and co-directs the Integrated Sustainable Design Masters Studio. He is also the Seidler Chair in the Practice of Architecture at the University of New South Wales (UNSW) Australia and serves on the Design Advisory Board of DesignSingapore Council.
