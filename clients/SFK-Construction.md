# SFK Construction

## Company Overview

SFK Construction is a leading construction and engineering company based in Hong Kong, specializing in civil engineering, building construction, foundation works, and infrastructure development. With decades of experience in the construction industry, SFK has established itself as a trusted contractor for both public and private sector projects throughout Hong Kong and the wider Asia-Pacific region.

## History

SFK Construction was established in the early 1980s in Hong Kong, initially focusing on local building projects. Through strategic growth and a commitment to quality, the company has evolved into a comprehensive construction group with capabilities spanning various construction disciplines.

Key milestones in SFK's history include:

- **1980s**: Foundation of the company and initial focus on residential building projects
- **1990s**: Expansion into civil engineering and infrastructure works
- **2000s**: Growth of portfolio to include major government contracts and MTR projects
- **2010s**: Development of specialized divisions and technical capabilities
- **Present**: Continued growth with focus on sustainable construction and innovative building technologies
