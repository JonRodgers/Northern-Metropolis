# Shanghai SUS Environment Co., Ltd.

## Company Overview

Shanghai SUS Environment Co., Ltd. (SUS Environment) is a leading Chinese environmental services company specializing in waste management, waste-to-energy solutions, and environmental engineering. Founded in 2000 and headquartered in Shanghai, the company has grown to become one of China's premier providers of integrated environmental services, with operations spanning waste incineration, landfill management, biomass energy, environmental remediation, and wastewater treatment.

## History and Development

SUS Environment was established in 2000 as a specialized environmental services provider in Shanghai. The company's development timeline includes:

- **2000**: Founding of Shanghai SUS Environment Co., Ltd.
- **2005**: Completion of first waste-to-energy facility in Shanghai
- **2010**: Expansion into multiple provinces across China
- **2015**: Listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (SSE)
- **2017**: Achieved processing capacity of over 20,000 tons of waste per day
- **2020**: Celebrated 20th anniversary with operations in over 15 provinces
- **Present**: Continues expansion with focus on technological innovation and international cooperation

## Core Business Areas

### Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) Treatment

- **Waste-to-Energy Plants**: Design, construction, and operation of modern incineration facilities that convert municipal waste into electricity
- **Mechanical Biological Treatment (MBT)**: Integrated systems for waste sorting, recycling, and biological treatment
- **Landfill Management**: Engineering, construction, and operation of sanitary landfills with leachate treatment and landfill gas recovery
- **Kitchen Waste Treatment**: Specialized facilities for processing food waste into biogas and organic fertilizer

### Industrial Waste Management

- **Hazardous Waste Treatment**: Safe disposal and treatment of industrial hazardous waste
- **Industrial Solid Waste Recycling**: Recovery of valuable materials from industrial byproducts
- **Specialized Industrial Waste Solutions**: Customized treatment processes for specific industrial sectors

### Environmental Engineering

- **Environmental Remediation**: Soil and groundwater contamination treatment
- **Wastewater Treatment**: Municipal and industrial wastewater processing facilities
- **Air Pollution Control**: Advanced systems for controlling emissions from industrial processes and waste treatment
- **Environmental Monitoring**: Comprehensive monitoring systems for air, water, and soil quality

### Renewable Energy

- **Biomass Power Generation**: Converting agricultural waste and forestry residues into clean energy
- **Landfill Gas Recovery**: Capturing and utilizing methane from landfills for energy production
- **Solar Integration**: Incorporating solar power into environmental facilities

## Technology and Innovation

SUS Environment has developed and implemented numerous proprietary technologies:

### Waste Incineration Technology

- **Grate Combustion Systems**: Advanced moving grate systems optimized for Chinese waste characteristics
- **Fluidized Bed Technology**: Specialized for certain waste streams with higher efficiency
- **Syngas Cleaning**: Advanced systems for purification of synthesis gas from gasification processes

### Flue Gas Treatment

- **SNCR/SCR DeNOx Systems**: Selective catalytic and non-catalytic reduction systems for nitrogen oxide control
- **Dry/Semi-Dry Scrubbers**: Advanced acid gas removal systems
- **Activated Carbon Injection**: For dioxin and heavy metal control
- **Bag Filter Systems**: High-efficiency particulate removal

### Leachate Treatment

- **Membrane Bioreactor (MBR)**: Advanced biological treatment combined with membrane filtration
- **Reverse Osmosis**: High-purity water recovery from leachate
- **Evaporation Systems**: Zero-liquid discharge solutions for challenging leachate streams

### Intelligent Operation Systems

- **Digital Twin Technology**: Virtual modeling of facilities for optimization
- **IoT-Based Monitoring**: Real-time data collection and analysis
- **AI-Driven Process Control**: Intelligent systems for optimizing combustion and environmental performance

## Environmental Standards and Compliance

SUS Environment adheres to stringent environmental standards:

- **Emission Standards**: Compliance with and often exceeding China's ultra-low emission standards (GB 18485-2014)
- **European Standards**: Many facilities designed to meet EU 2010/75/EU Directive requirements
