# MACE Group - BIM Services Proposal

## Executive Summary

**Proposal Date:** May 25, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Atmospheres (www.atmospheres.hk)

## 2. Proposed Scope of Services

### 2.1 Rail Projects - Philippines

**Project Context:**
- MACE's rail infrastructure initiatives in the Philippines
- Complex coordination requirements across multiple disciplines
- Need for integrated BIM models for planning and execution

**Atmospheres BIM Services:**
- **BIM Strategy & Planning**
  - Development of BIM Execution Plan (BEP)
  - Information requirements definition aligned with project phases
  - Coordination protocols and clash detection procedures

- **3D Modeling & Coordination**
  - Comprehensive 3D BIM model development
  - Multi-disciplinary coordination (civil, structural, MEP, architectural)
  - Clash detection and resolution management
  - Model quality assurance and validation

- **Design Integration**
  - Parametric modeling for design optimization
  - Constructability analysis and value engineering
  - Phasing and sequencing visualization

- **Documentation & Deliverables**
  - Automated drawing generation from BIM models
  - Specifications and schedules extraction
  - Construction documentation packages

### 2.2 Data Centre Project

**Project Context:**
- Mission-critical infrastructure requiring precision coordination
- Complex MEP systems integration
- Sustainability and operational efficiency requirements

**Atmospheres BIM Services:**
- **Detailed BIM Modeling**
  - Comprehensive data centre infrastructure modeling
  - MEP systems integration and coordination
  - Cable routing and infrastructure planning
  - Thermal and airflow analysis visualization

- **Operational BIM**
  - Asset management model development
  - Maintenance access planning
  - Facility management documentation
  - As-built BIM for handover

- **Performance Analysis**
  - Energy efficiency modeling and optimization
  - Sustainability performance tracking
  - Operational cost analysis through BIM

- **Coordination & Clash Management**
  - Real-time coordination with design and construction teams
  - Automated clash detection and reporting
  - Resolution tracking and documentation

## 3. Service Delivery Model

### 3.1 Engagement Structure

**Phase 1: Discovery & Planning (Weeks 1-2)**
- Project requirements assessment
- BIM Execution Plan development
- Team mobilization and tool setup
- Stakeholder alignment workshops

**Phase 2: Model Development (Ongoing)**
- Concurrent BIM modeling with design development
- Weekly coordination meetings
- Clash detection and resolution cycles
- Model updates and refinements

**Phase 3: Documentation & Handover (Project Dependent)**
- Construction documentation generation
- As-built model updates
- Facility management model preparation
- Training and knowledge transfer

### 3.2 Collaboration & Communication

- Dedicated BIM coordination team
- Weekly progress meetings and coordination sessions
- Cloud-based model sharing and version control
- Real-time clash detection and reporting
- Integration with MACE's project management systems
