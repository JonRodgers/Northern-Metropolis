# Leighton Asia

## Company Overview

Leighton Asia is a leading international contractor operating across Asia. As part of the CIMIC Group, Leighton Asia has established itself as a premier construction and engineering company with a strong presence in Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines, and India. With over 45 years of experience in the region, the company has built a reputation for delivering complex and challenging projects across diverse sectors.

## History

Leighton Asia was established in Hong Kong in 1975 as an extension of the Australian-based Leighton Holdings (now CIMIC Group). Since its inception, the company has grown significantly, expanding its operations throughout Asia and delivering landmark projects that have shaped the region's infrastructure and skyline.

In 2016, as part of a global rebranding, Leighton Holdings was renamed CIMIC Group (Construction, Infrastructure, Mining and Concessions), with Leighton Asia continuing as a key operational company within the group's construction division.
