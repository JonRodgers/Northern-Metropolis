# Tecno

## Company Overview

Tecno is an Italian furniture company founded in 1953 by the Borsani brothers, Osvaldo and Fulgenzio. Headquartered in Milan, Tecno has established itself as a pioneer in the design and manufacturing of high-end furniture and architectural partition systems for office, public, and residential environments. The company is renowned for its innovative approach to industrial design, combining technological advancement with elegant aesthetics.

## History & Heritage

### Founding Years (1950s-1960s)

Tecno emerged from the Borsani family's furniture-making tradition, which dated back to the early 20th century. Under the leadership of Osvaldo Borsani, an architect and designer, the company quickly distinguished itself through its commitment to industrial production methods and modern design principles.

The iconic P40 adjustable lounge chair (1955) and D70 sofa (1954) exemplified Tecno's early approach, featuring innovative mechanical systems that allowed for multiple configurations and positions—a revolutionary concept at the time.

### Expansion & Innovation (1970s-1980s)

During this period, Tecno expanded its focus to office furniture and systems, collaborating with renowned designers like Eugenio Gerli, Eduardo Vittoria, and Marco Fantoni. The company introduced groundbreaking products such as the Graphis office system (1968) and the Modus office furniture range (1972), which redefined workplace environments with their modular and flexible designs.

### Contemporary Evolution (1990s-Present)

In recent decades, Tecno has continued to evolve, embracing digital technologies and smart systems. The company has expanded internationally while maintaining its commitment to Italian craftsmanship and design excellence. Notable contemporary projects include the Nomos table system by Norman Foster, the Clavis system by Daniele Del Missier and Centro Progetti Tecno, and the innovative IO.T (Internet of Things) solutions for connected workspaces.

## Design Philosophy

Tecno's approach to design is characterized by:

- **Technological Innovation**: Pioneering new materials, mechanisms, and production techniques
- **Functional Elegance**: Creating products that combine utility with refined aesthetics
- **Systematic Thinking**: Developing comprehensive, modular systems rather than isolated pieces
- **Industrial Precision**: Employing rigorous manufacturing standards and attention to detail
- **Architectural Integration**: Designing furniture that harmonizes with and enhances architectural spaces

## Signature Products

### Office Systems

- **Graphis**: Modular office system with aluminum framework and customizable components
- **Nomos**: Minimalist table system designed by Norman Foster featuring die-cast aluminum joints
- **Clavis**: Contemporary workstation system with integrated technology and flexible configurations
- **Beta**: Workplace system with adaptable elements for evolving office environments
- **Vara**: Modular seating system for public and corporate spaces

### Architectural Partitions

- **W80**: High-performance sound-insulating partition system
- **WE**: Elegant double-glazed partition system with minimal profiles
- **WA**: Adaptable partition system for flexible space division
- **IO.T**: Smart partition system with integrated technological features

### Iconic Seating

- **P40**: Adjustable lounge chair with multiple configurations (1955)
- **D70**: Transformable sofa system (1954)
- **Sella**: Office chair with innovative ergonomic features
- **Heron**: Executive chair collection with distinctive silhouette
