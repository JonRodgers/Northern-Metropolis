The CPC Central Committee and the State Council on promoting Opinions on High-Quality Urban Development
(August 15, 2025)

https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_12266/202509/content_7039600.html

Cities are important carriers of modernization and vital spaces for people's well-being. my country's urbanization is transitioning from a period of rapid growth to a period of stable development, and urban development is shifting from a phase of large-scale incremental expansion to one focused on improving the quality and efficiency of existing resources. To accelerate the transformation of urban development patterns and promote high-quality urban development, the following opinions are hereby proposed.

I. General Requirements

Guided by Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, we will thoroughly implement the spirit of the 20th CPC National Congress and the Second and Third Plenary Sessions of the 20th CPC Central Committee, fully implement General Secretary Xi Jinping's important expositions on urban work, implement the spirit of the Central Urban Work Conference, uphold and strengthen the Party's overall leadership, earnestly practice the concept of people-centered cities, adhere to the general principle of seeking progress while maintaining stability, and adhere to adapting to local conditions and providing classified guidance. With the goal of building an innovative, livable, beautiful, resilient, civilized, and intelligent modern people-centered city, and with the theme of promoting high-quality urban development, we will focus on the intrinsic development of cities, take urban renewal as an important lever, vigorously promote urban structural optimization, energy transformation, quality improvement, green transformation, cultural heritage preservation, and governance efficiency, firmly safeguard the bottom line of urban safety, and forge a new path of urban modernization with Chinese characteristics.

In our work, we must: transform the concept of urban development and pay more attention to people-centeredness; transform the mode of urban development and pay more attention to intensive and efficient development; transform the driving force of urban development and pay more attention to distinctive development; transform the focus of urban work and pay more attention to governance investment; and transform the methods of urban work and pay more attention to overall coordination.

The main objectives are: by 2030, significant progress will be made in the construction of modern people-oriented cities, policies and systems adapted to high-quality urban development will be continuously improved, the transformation of old and new growth drivers will be accelerated, the quality of life will be significantly improved, green transformation will be deeply promoted, the safety foundation will be effectively consolidated, cultural charm will be fully demonstrated, and the level of governance will be greatly improved; by 2035, modern people-oriented cities will be basically completed.

II. Optimizing the Modern Urban System

(I) Steadily and orderly promote the integrated development of urban agglomerations and metropolitan areas. Develop cluster-style and networked modern urban agglomerations and metropolitan areas, and build a rationally laid-out modern urban system. Support the development of world-class urban agglomerations in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region, the Yangtze River Delta, and the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area; promote the Chengdu-Chongqing Twin-City Economic Circle and the Yangtze River Midstream Urban Agglomeration to become high-quality development growth poles; enhance the supporting role of urban agglomerations and metropolitan areas in the central and western regions and the northeast in regional coordinated development; and promote complementary positioning, interconnected infrastructure, and collaborative governance among cities. Strengthen industrial chain collaboration within urban agglomerations and optimize the division of labor and spatial connections between urban agglomerations. Develop and expand modern metropolitan areas; support qualified areas to promote integrated development; establish and improve the institutional mechanisms for integrated development; deepen the reform of the appropriate separation of economic zones and administrative regions; and promote convenient and efficient commuting, tiered industrial support, and convenient sharing of public services.

(II) Enhancing the Comprehensive Competitiveness of Mega-Cities and Super-Large Cities. Mega-cities and super-large cities will be encouraged to strengthen and refine their core functions in accordance with the functional positioning approved by the state, control their size, and rationally and orderly relocate non-core functions to become the main engines of high-quality development. Mega-cities and super-large cities will be supported in promoting institutional innovation based on their actual conditions. Some megacities and super-large cities will be supported in enhancing their ability to allocate high-end global production factors. Mega-cities and super-large cities will be supported in establishing science and technology innovation platforms and bases to enhance their original and disruptive science and technology innovation capabilities. Provinces with suitable conditions will be encouraged to cultivate and develop sub-provincial center cities.

(III) Enhancing the carrying capacity of small and medium-sized cities and county towns. Promote small and medium-sized cities to dynamically optimize infrastructure layout and public service supply in accordance with resident population trends, and to steadily and prudently optimize administrative division settings according to procedures. Promote urbanization with county towns as important carriers in a categorized manner, and vigorously develop the county economy. Develop small towns according to local conditions. Adjust and expand the economic and social management authority of county-level cities and large towns with large economic scale and rapid population growth in a timely manner. Promote the transformation and development of cities with continuous population outflow and resource-based cities. Support border towns in enhancing their capacity for border stability, population agglomeration, and safe development. Plan and construct urban and rural infrastructure and public service systems in a coordinated manner to promote integrated urban-rural development. Continuously promote the urbanization of the agricultural population.

III. Cultivating and Strengthening New Drivers of Urban Development

(IV) Enhance Urban Development Momentum Through City-Specific Policies. Based on the city's resource endowment and basic conditions, carefully cultivate an innovation ecosystem and stimulate innovation and entrepreneurship. Strengthen original innovation and key core technology research, enhance the synergy between technological innovation and industrial innovation, and coordinate the transformation and upgrading of traditional industries, the cultivation and expansion of emerging industries, and the layout and construction of future industries, developing new productive forces according to local conditions. Accelerate the construction of an international science and technology innovation center and a regional science and technology innovation center. Deepen the implementation of the national strategic emerging industry cluster development project. Cultivate and expand advanced manufacturing clusters. Develop intelligent construction, cultivate a modern construction industry chain, and accelerate the transformation and upgrading of the construction industry. Vigorously develop productive service industries and promote the improvement of the quality and level of consumer service industries. Develop the first-launch economy, the silver economy, the ice and snow economy, and the low-altitude economy, and cultivate new consumption scenarios. Deepen the construction of an international consumption center city.

(V) Activating the potential of existing urban resources. Conduct a comprehensive inventory of urban assets and resources, including housing, facilities, and land. Renovate and utilize existing buildings such as old factories, inefficient buildings, and idle traditional commercial facilities, and establish a planning adjustment mechanism adapted to the needs of building function transformation and mixed-use development. Strengthen and standardize the management of existing urban infrastructure assets to improve operational efficiency. Optimize policies for property rights collection, integration, exchange, and registration, and revitalize and utilize existing inefficient land.

(VI) Establish a sustainable urban construction and operation investment and financing system. Implement the primary responsibility of local governments and coordinate funding channels including fiscal funds, social capital, and financial institutions. For projects involving major safety and basic livelihood guarantees, the central government will increase special transfer payments, and central budgetary investments will provide appropriate subsidies. Innovate fiscal and financial policy tools to attract and regulate social capital participation, and steadily and prudently promote public utility price reforms. Strengthen the supply of medium- and long-term credit, leverage the financing functions of the bond and stock markets, and encourage insurance, trust, and other institutions to provide financial services. Accelerate the reform and transformation of financing platforms and resolutely curb new local government hidden debt.

(VII) Enhance the city's level of opening up and cooperation. Optimize the city's open environment and services, and create an international urban living space. Build an international comprehensive transportation hub city. Support qualified cities in undertaking major foreign affairs and diplomatic activities, and attract influential international organizations to settle in the city. In conjunction with the high-quality joint construction of the "Belt and Road," build a number of international gateway hub cities and regional open node cities. Strengthen international exchanges and cooperation in the field of human settlements, and continuously enhance the influence of World Cities Day and the Global Sustainable Cities Award (Shanghai Award).

IV. Creating High-Quality Urban Living Spaces

(VIII) Systematically Promote the Construction of "Good Houses" and Complete Communities. Accelerate the construction of a new model for real estate development to better meet the rigid and diversified housing needs of the people. Improve the design, construction, maintenance, and service levels of housing across the entire chain, and vigorously promote the construction of safe, comfortable, green, and smart "good houses." Implement an action plan to improve the quality of property services. Steadily promote the renovation of urban villages and dilapidated houses, and support the independent renewal and reconstruction of old houses. Continuously promote the renovation of old urban communities. Adhere to the integrated planning of population, industry, urban areas, and transportation, and build innovative industrial communities and business communities. Accelerate the construction of complete communities, improve embedded service facilities in urban communities, and build convenient living circles in cities. Scientifically formulate and implement special plans for urban renewal, promote urban physical examination and urban renewal in an integrated manner, create a good urban environment that is suitable for work, living, leisure, and tourism, and promote the integration of industry and city and the balance between work and residence.

(ix) Improve urban infrastructure. Optimize the layout, structure, and function of urban infrastructure, and strengthen system integration. Make efficient use of urban underground space and optimize the urban spatial structure. Accelerate the construction and renovation of urban underground pipeline networks, and construct underground integrated pipe corridors according to local conditions. Strengthen the coverage of fifth-generation mobile communication networks (5G) and gigabit optical networks, optimize the layout of computing facilities, and promote the construction of new urban infrastructure. Optimize the urban water resource allocation and water supply security management system. Improve the modern comprehensive transportation system, enhance intercity and intracity commuting efficiency, improve the layout of transportation hub transfer facilities, and strengthen the construction of convenient facilities such as parking spaces and charging piles.

(X) Improve the quality and balance of public services. Establish a sound and efficient public service system for employment. Improve the social security system for flexible employment personnel, rural migrant workers, and those in new employment forms. Accelerate the construction and renovation of age-friendly, child-friendly, and barrier-free facilities. Expand the supply of inclusive elderly care services and promote the integration of medical and elderly care. Improve the inclusive childcare service system and vigorously develop integrated childcare and early childhood education services. Optimize the allocation of educational resources, implement the action plan for expanding and improving the quality of basic education, and promote the construction of learning cities. Vigorously implement the basic medical and health care strengthening project, build closely integrated medical alliances, and promote the expansion and downward flow of high-quality medical resources and their balanced regional distribution. Develop affordable housing according to local conditions. Steadily promote the participation of flexible employment personnel in the housing provident fund system. Strengthen social security and tiered and categorized social assistance to ensure a safety net for people's livelihoods.

V. Promote the green and low-carbon transformation of urban development

(xi) Promote energy conservation and carbon reduction in the production sector. Implement industrial energy efficiency and water efficiency improvement initiatives, and promote green, low-carbon, and advanced technologies and equipment. Strengthen the management and control of high-energy-consuming and high-emission projects, and promote the orderly withdrawal of outdated production capacity. Establish a carbon footprint labeling and certification system for key products. Establish a centralized energy supply system for industrial parks, and promote full coverage of wastewater, waste heat, and solid waste co-processing. Vigorously develop green buildings, accelerate the research and application of new building materials, promote the large-scale development of ultra-low energy consumption buildings and low-carbon buildings, and increase the efforts in energy-saving renovation of existing buildings.

(XII) Practice a green and low-carbon lifestyle. Deepen the implementation of the national water conservation action and comprehensively promote the construction of water-saving cities. Promote clean heating in northern regions during winter. Promote energy-saving and low-carbon household products and reduce consumption of disposable consumer goods and packaging materials. Continue to promote the "Clean Plate Campaign". Deepen the classification of urban household waste and promote the integration of classification outlets with recycling outlets. Prioritize the development of urban public transportation and advocate green and low-carbon travel.

(XIII) Strengthen urban ecological environment governance. Enhance the implementation of ecological environment zoning control. Deepen the implementation of urban ecological restoration projects. Improve the urban park and greenway network system, construct pocket parks, and promote the open sharing of green spaces. Promote coordinated emission reduction of air pollutants in key cities and surrounding areas to continuously improve air quality. Strengthen the control of urban noise pollution and restaurant fumes. Strengthen the control of emerging pollutants such as antibiotics and microplastics. Promote the resource utilization and disposal of construction waste. Promote full coverage of urban domestic sewage pipe networks, consolidate the achievements in the treatment of urban black and odorous water bodies, and build happy and beautiful rivers and lakes.

VI. Enhance urban safety resilience

(XIV) Strengthen the safety management of housing and municipal infrastructure. Establish a full life-cycle safety management system for housing, including housing safety inspections, housing safety management funds, and housing quality and safety insurance. Strictly limit super high-rise buildings and enhance high-altitude fire protection capabilities. Implement the urban prefabricated housing renovation and improvement campaign and the reinforcement project for housing facilities in earthquake-prone areas. Strengthen the rectification of shared rental housing. Accelerate the construction of safety projects for urban infrastructure lifelines. Promote the distributed layout of important facilities, energy, and water sources, improve disaster resistance standards, and enhance safety assurance capabilities.

(XV) Strengthen safety management in key industries and sectors. Strictly implement the safety responsibility system, adhere to the principle of shared responsibility between the Party and the government, and ensure that those who manage an industry, business, or production and operation must also manage its safety. Urge production and operation units to fulfill their primary responsibility for safety. Focusing on hazardous chemicals, special equipment, construction, urban gas, road traffic, and fire safety, implement graded, intelligent, and precise control over major risk sources, and routinely promote the investigation and rectification of safety hazards and safety training for employees. Establish a credit evaluation system for enterprise safety production and emphasize the application of evaluation results.

(XVI) Improve the level of urban public health prevention, control, and treatment. Promote the construction of national regional public health centers. Strengthen the prevention and control capabilities of national major infectious disease prevention and control bases, national emergency medical rescue bases, and designated infectious disease hospitals, and build a tiered and triaged treatment system. Strengthen the reserve of emergency medical supplies. Promote multi-departmental and cross-city collaboration, and strengthen public health emergency management and joint prevention and control. Improve the mental health service system.

(XVII) Enhance the city's comprehensive disaster prevention, mitigation, and relief capabilities. Establish an urban safety risk spectrum and improve the normalized risk and hazard monitoring, early warning, investigation, and rectification mechanism. Optimize the urban disaster prevention and mitigation spatial pattern, increase ecological barriers and isolation evacuation channels, and reserve flexible space for disaster prevention and relief. Improve the flood control and drainage mechanism and enhance the city's drainage and flood control capabilities. Make comprehensive use of above-ground and underground space, strengthen the construction of public infrastructure for both normal and emergency use, and build a system of emergency shelters with sufficient quantity and reasonable layout. Develop scientific emergency plans for major disasters. Strengthen the construction of emergency command, response, and rescue systems, and promote the pre-positioning and stockpiling of emergency rescue and relief equipment and materials.

VII. Promoting the prosperity and development of urban culture

Improve the system for the general survey and investigation of urban cultural heritage, and conduct special surveys of old cities and their historical blocks.
