# Huizhou

## Overview

Huizhou (惠州) is an industrial city in eastern Guangdong Province, strategically located between Guangzhou and Shenzhen. With a growing manufacturing sector and improving infrastructure, Huizhou is becoming increasingly important to the GBA's economic integration.

## Key Information

- **Population**: Approximately 6 million
- **Location**: Eastern Guangdong, between Guangzhou and Shenzhen
- **Status**: Prefecture-level city
- **Development Stage**: Rapid industrial growth

## Economic Significance

### Major Industries
- Electronics and semiconductors
- Petrochemicals and refining
- Machinery and equipment
- Textiles and apparel
- Food processing

### Manufacturing Hub
- Growing electronics manufacturing
- Petrochemical production
- Export-oriented industries
- Technology integration focus

## Infrastructure

### Transportation
- Huizhou Airport
- Highway connections to Guangzhou and Shenzhen
- Port facilities at Daya Bay
- Rail network development

### Development Zones
- Huizhou Economic and Technological Development Zone
- Daya Bay Economic and Technological Development Zone
- Huicheng District - commercial center

## Strategic Importance

- Bridge between Guangzhou and Shenzhen
- Integration with GBA initiatives
- Cross-regional cooperation opportunities
- Industrial development acceleration

## Natural Resources

- Daya Bay coastal area
- Natural harbors and port facilities
- Agricultural lands
- Forest and ecological areas

## Future Development

- Industrial upgrading and modernization
- Technology and innovation focus
- Port and logistics development
- Regional economic integration
