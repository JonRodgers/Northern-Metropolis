# Zhuhai

## Overview

Zhuhai (珠海) is a coastal city in southern Guangdong Province, known for its beautiful seaside environment and strategic location. As one of China's earliest Special Economic Zones, Zhuhai has developed into a modern city with a focus on quality of life and sustainable development.

## Key Information

- **Population**: Approximately 2.4 million
- **Location**: Southern Guangdong, bordering Macau
- **Status**: Special Economic Zone (SEZ)
- **Established as SEZ**: 1979

## Economic Significance

### Major Industries
- Manufacturing and processing
- Tourism and hospitality
- Logistics and port operations
- Electronics and appliances
- Pharmaceutical and chemical industries

### Strategic Location
- Gateway to Macau
- Port facilities for international trade
- Cross-border cooperation opportunities
- Hengqin Cooperation Zone (Zhuhai-Macau)

## Infrastructure

### Transportation
- Zhuhai Airport
- Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge connection
- Port facilities and maritime transport
- Road networks to neighboring cities

### Development Zones
- Hengqin Cooperation Zone - Zhuhai-Macau integration
- Qingwan District - emerging development area
- Xiangzhou District - commercial center

## Tourism & Lifestyle

- Beautiful coastal scenery
- Beach resorts and leisure facilities
- International golf courses
- Quality of life focus
- Environmental conservation

## Culture & Heritage

- Portuguese colonial influence (proximity to Macau)
- Modern urban planning
- International business environment
- Diverse cultural attractions

## Future Development

- Deepening Macau-Zhuhai integration
- Tourism and leisure expansion
- Sustainable city development
- Cross-border cooperation initiatives
