# Zhongshan

## Overview

Zhongshan (中山) is a city in southern Guangdong Province, strategically located in the Pearl River Delta. Named after Dr. Sun Yat-sen, Zhongshan combines industrial development with cultural heritage and is increasingly integrated into the GBA's economic framework.

## Key Information

- **Population**: Approximately 3.4 million
- **Location**: Southern Guangdong, between Guangzhou and Zhuhai
- **Status**: Prefecture-level city
- **Named After**: Dr. Sun Yat-sen (revolutionary leader)
