# Northern Metropolis

## Overview

The Northern Metropolis is Hong Kong's ambitious new development initiative, representing a strategic expansion into the New Territories. This major urban development project aims to create a new economic and innovation hub that integrates Hong Kong with Shenzhen and Guangdong Province.

## Key Information

- **Location**: Hong Kong's New Territories, bordering Shenzhen
- **Status**: Major development initiative
- **Planned Population**: Approximately 2.5 million
- **Development Timeline**: Long-term strategic project

## Strategic Objectives

### Economic Integration
- Deepening Hong Kong-Shenzhen cooperation
- Cross-border business facilitation
- Regional economic integration
- International competitiveness enhancement

### Innovation Focus

- Biotech and life sciences
- Advanced manufacturing
