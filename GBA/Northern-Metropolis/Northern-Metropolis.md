## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Northern Metropolis represents Hong Kong's most ambitious urban development initiative since the 1980s. Announced in the 2022 Policy Address by Chief Executive John Lee, this strategic expansion aims to transform Hong Kong's New Territories into a world-class metropolitan area integrating with Shenzhen and the Greater Bay Area.

**Key Facts:**
- **Planned Population:** 2.5 million residents
- **Development Area:** 900+ km² (including Lok Ma Chau Loop, Heung Yuen Wai, Fanling/Sheung Shui, Kwu Tung North)
- **Estimated Investment:** HK$750 billion+ (USD $95 billion+)
- **Timeline:** 2026-2050+ (phased development)
- **Strategic Focus:** Innovation, technology, cross-border integration, sustainability
- **Government Lead:** Hong Kong SAR Government, Development Bureau, Planning Department
- **Key Partners:** Shenzhen Municipal Government, Guangdong Province, private developers

### 2. Northern Metropolis Urban Design Guidelines

**Density & Built Form:**
- [ ] Mixed-use development with residential, commercial, office
- [ ] Building heights: 20-40 stories in commercial zones, 15-25 in residential
- [ ] Floor Area Ratio (FAR): 4-8 in commercial zones, 2-4 in residential
- [ ] Ground floor activation with retail and public uses
- [ ] Setbacks and building articulation for visual interest

**Public Realm & Open Spaces:**
- [ ] Minimum 20% of development area as public open space
- [ ] Connected network of parks, plazas, and green corridors
- [ ] Pedestrian-friendly streetscapes with wide sidewalks
- [ ] Street trees and landscaping
- [ ] Public art and cultural spaces
- [ ] Accessible design for all users

**Sustainability Standards:**
- [ ] LEED Gold or equivalent for all buildings
- [ ] Net-zero energy buildings (all new construction)
- [ ] Green roofs on 50%+ of buildings
- [ ] Rainwater harvesting and greywater recycling
- [ ] 90% waste diversion during construction
- [ ] Circular economy principles in material selection

**Transport & Connectivity:**
- [ ] Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) within 500m of MTR
- [ ] Pedestrian and cycling networks
- [ ] Electric vehicle charging infrastructure
- [ ] Public transport priority
- [ ] Cross-border connectivity and seamless movement
- [ ] Smart mobility solutions

**Heritage & Culture:**
- [ ] Preservation of significant heritage sites
- [ ] Integration of local culture and history
- [ ] Public art and cultural programming
- [ ] Community spaces and gathering places
- [ ] Educational and cultural facilities

**Environmental Protection:**
- [ ] Biodiversity protection and habitat restoration
- [ ] Wetland and water system protection
- [ ] Air quality and noise management
- [ ] Light pollution control
- [ ] Climate resilience and adaptation
