# Guangzhou

## Overview

Guangzhou (广州), the capital of Guangdong Province, is one of the most important economic and cultural centers in southern China. As a major historical trading port and modern metropolis, Guangzhou serves as a gateway between China and the world.

## Key Information

- **Population**: Approximately 15 million
- **Location**: Northern part of the Pearl River Delta
- **Status**: Provincial capital and sub-provincial city
- **Established**: Over 2,200 years of history as a trading port

## Economic Significance

### Major Industries
- International trade and commerce
- Manufacturing and processing
- Logistics and transportation
- Finance and banking
- Tourism and hospitality

### Trade Hub
- Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair) - world's largest trade fair
- Major port facilities on the Pearl River
- International business center

## Infrastructure

### Transportation
- Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport
- High-speed rail connections to major cities
- Extensive metro system
- Pearl River waterway transport

### Development Zones
- Guangzhou Economic and Technological Development Zone
- Nansha District - emerging development area
- Huangpu District - central business district

## Culture & Heritage

- Ancient trading history along the Silk Road
- Traditional Cantonese culture
- Historic temples and cultural sites
- Modern museums and cultural institutions

## Future Development

- Integration with GBA initiatives
- Technology and innovation focus
- Sustainable urban development
- Enhanced regional connectivity
