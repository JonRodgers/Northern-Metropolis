## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Shenzhen (深圳) has transformed from a fishing village of 30,000 people in 1979 to a global megacity of 12+ million residents and a world-leading technology and innovation hub. As China's first Special Economic Zone and a pilot for economic reform, Shenzhen continues to drive innovation, technological advancement, and urban development at an unprecedented scale.

**Key Facts:**
- **Population:** 12+ million residents
- **Area:** 1,997 km² (including Qianhai expansion)
- **GDP:** RMB 3.2 trillion+ (USD $450 billion+) - 4th largest in China
- **Status:** Special Economic Zone (SEZ), Pilot Free Trade Zone, Innovation Hub
- **Government Lead:** Shenzhen Municipal Government, Planning and Natural Resources Bureau
- **Key Focus:** Technology, innovation, fintech, advanced manufacturing, green development
- **Major Companies:** Huawei, Tencent, DJI, BYD, Ping An Insurance, China Vanke, Country Garden
