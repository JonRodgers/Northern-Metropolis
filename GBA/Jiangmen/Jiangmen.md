# Jiangmen

## Overview

Jiangmen (江门) is an emerging industrial city in western Guangdong Province. With a growing manufacturing sector and strategic location, Jiangmen is increasingly important to the GBA's economic development and regional integration.

## Key Information

- **Population**: Approximately 3.5 million
- **Location**: Western Guangdong, near Guangzhou and Zhuhai
- **Status**: Prefecture-level city
- **Development Stage**: Rapid industrialization

## Economic Significance

### Major Industries
- Electronics and electrical equipment
- Machinery and equipment manufacturing
- Textiles and apparel
- Food processing and beverages
- Chemical and pharmaceutical products

### Growth Sectors
- Industrial manufacturing expansion
- Export-oriented production
- Technology integration
- Emerging innovation sectors

## Infrastructure

### Transportation
- Jiangmen Airport
- Highway connections to major cities
- Port facilities for maritime trade
- Rail network development

### Development Zones
- Jiangmen Economic and Technological Development Zone
- Pengjiang District - commercial center
- Xinhui District - industrial hub

## Strategic Importance

- Gateway to western Guangdong
- Integration with GBA initiatives
- Cross-regional cooperation opportunities
- Belt and Road Initiative participation

## Culture & Heritage

- Traditional Cantonese culture
- Historic temples and sites
- Modern urban development
- Local culinary traditions

## Future Development

- Industrial upgrading and modernization
- Technology and innovation focus
- Regional connectivity enhancement
- Sustainable economic growth
