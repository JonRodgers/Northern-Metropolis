## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Hong Kong (香港) is a Special Administrative Region of China and one of the world's leading global financial centers. With a population of 7.5 million and a unique "One Country, Two Systems" governance model, Hong Kong continues to drive innovation, financial services, and urban development at a global scale.

Beyond the Northern Metropolis initiative, Hong Kong has numerous ongoing and planned development projects across the territory, including waterfront redevelopment, heritage conservation, smart city infrastructure, and sustainable urban renewal. These initiatives represent significant opportunities for architects, designers, BIM specialists, project managers, and smart city solution providers.

**Key Facts:**
- **Population:** 7.5 million residents
- **Area:** 1,104 km² (Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, New Territories)
- **GDP:** USD $370 billion+ (6th largest in Asia)
- **Status:** Special Administrative Region (SAR), Global Financial Center
- **Government Lead:** Hong Kong SAR Government, Development Bureau, Planning Department
- **Key Focus:** Financial services, innovation, sustainability, smart city, heritage conservation

## BIM & DIGITAL TWIN INITIATIVES

### 1. Hong Kong Government BIM Strategy

**BIM Mandate & Requirements:**
- [ ] All government-funded projects > HK$30 million require BIM
- [ ] BIM Level 2 minimum (coordinated 3D models)
- [ ] IFC format for data exchange
- [ ] COBie data for asset management
- [ ] BIM execution plans required

**BIM Standards & Guidelines:**
