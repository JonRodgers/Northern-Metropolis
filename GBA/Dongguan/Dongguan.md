# Dongguan

## Overview

Dongguan (东莞) is a major manufacturing city in central Guangdong Province, famous as the "world's factory." With a highly developed manufacturing sector and strategic location, Dongguan is a crucial economic engine of the Pearl River Delta and the GBA.

## Key Information

- **Population**: Approximately 8.5 million
- **Location**: Central Guangdong, between Guangzhou and Shenzhen
- **Status**: Prefecture-level city
- **Nickname**: "World's Factory"

## Economic Significance

- Furniture manufacturing

### Manufacturing Powerhouse
